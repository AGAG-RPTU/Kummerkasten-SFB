Privacy statement

The Kummerkasten is built to store as little about you as possible. Messages are encrypted in your browser before they are sent. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking services and loads nothing from other servers.

3. Hosting and access logs

The site is hosted by netcup GmbH, Emmy-Noether-Straße 10, 76131 Karlsruhe, Germany, as a processor. Their web server logs, for every request, the IP address, date and time, the requested address, the status code, the amount of data transferred, the previously visited page (if the browser sends it) and the browser identification. The purpose is secure operation of the site; the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(e) GDPR. The logs are deleted after about 15 days. The application itself stores no IP addresses.

4. Messages

Category, subject, text and the optional name and contact details are encrypted in your browser. Only you, with your codeword, and the trusted persons listed on the front page can decrypt them; whoever runs the server cannot.

The server stores: the encrypted messages, an identifier derived from the codeword, a number for the conversation, the time of each message rounded to the nearest hour, who wrote it (you or which trusted person), and its length rounded up to 512 characters.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give by sending; if your message contains special categories of personal data, such as health data, also Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting the conversation.

5. Retention

Conversations are deleted automatically: 30 days after the trusted persons marked them as resolved, and at the latest 365 days after the last message. With your codeword you can delete your conversation yourself at any time; the trusted persons can delete it jointly. The hosting provider's backups may contain the encrypted data for a limited time.

6. Notifications

The trusted persons are informed about new messages by email. These emails contain only the conversation number, no content.

7. Spam protection

Before sending, your browser solves a computational task. For this the server stores only a random task identifier until it expires, after at most two hours.

8. Storage in your browser

Your language choice is kept in your browser's local storage and not transmitted. Your codeword is not stored anywhere.

9. Trusted persons

With their consent, the trusted persons' names, email addresses and public keys are public, so that anyone can check who can read messages.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and objection (Art. 21), and to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)). As messages are stored anonymously and encrypted, we cannot link them to you without your codeword; with the codeword you can view and delete your conversation yourself.
